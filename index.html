About the Camper

Our 6x10' "no build" camper trailer is your ticket to a minimalist outdoor adventure, equipped with everything you need for a comfortable stay. Designed for simplicity and functionality, this camper comes with a 2" ball hitch for easy towing and a range of features to enhance your camping experience:

  • Power & Charging: 200W solar panel, 1500W sinewave inverter, 110AH battery, 10-port 15Amp power strip, and an extension cord for shore power to keep your devices charged.
  • Cooling & Lighting: Two USB-powered fans for ventilation and dimming puck lighting to set the perfect ambiance.
  • Entertainment & Monitoring: 32" LCD Roku TV for streaming your favorite shows and an iPad display monitoring system to keep tabs on power usage.
  • Cooking & Cleaning: A 2-burner propane camp stove for meals and a wash sink for easy cleanup.
  • Comfort & Convenience: Twin-size bed, foldable table and chair for dining or working, and a clothing hanging rack for organization.

Perfect for solo travelers or couples seeking a no-fuss camping experience, this camper is ready to take you to your next destination.
